Spawning oss:auditor for self-review (both the initial round and a later needs-second-pass
round), I asked it to "report one verdict per checklist class" but never told it to open its
final message with `FINDINGS: <n>` / `NO FINDINGS`, the sentinel `scripts/review_return.py`
actually parses. Both times the auditor's message was substantively complete and, read directly,
unambiguous (it stated a real finding in full in round 1, and stated "0 findings" across every
class in round 2) -- but the classifier scored round 1's message `referred-not-stated` (its
trailing "Summary: ... 1 finding" line looks like a back-reference to the classifier, even though
the finding was fully stated 30 lines earlier in the same message) and round 2's as
`could-not-classify` (no header, no sentinel, no back-reference the tool recognises).

Cost: two manual re-reads to confirm by hand what the classifier could not tell me, and a
mismatch between "what the classifier says happened" and "what actually happened" that I had to
reconcile and explain in my own report rather than being able to trust the tool's verdict.

Root cause, as far as I could tell: `agents/developer/review.md` instructs asking BOTH spawns for
the FINDINGS:n/NO FINDINGS sentinel, but I only put that instruction in the Explore brief, not the
oss:auditor brief -- I assumed the auditor's own "one verdict per class" checklist shape was
sufficient, since its agent definition already asks for structured output. It is not: the
classifier only recognises the one sentinel shape regardless of which agent is asked.
