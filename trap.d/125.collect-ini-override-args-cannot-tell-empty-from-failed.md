RectorRunner::collectIniOverrideArgs() (#125) does `foreach (\ini_get_all(null, true) ?: [] as ...)`.
The `?: []` collapses two different situations into the identical empty result: "genuinely no
ini directive differs from its php.ini master" (nothing to forward, correct) and "ini_get_all()
itself returned something falsy" (could not enumerate directives at all). Either way
spawnProcWorker() silently proceeds with zero -d flags spliced into the standby worker's
proc_open() command, and nothing downstream -- no log line, no field on the daemon's own
diagnostics -- distinguishes the two.

Flagged by the oss:auditor self-review spawn on this diff (ranked `fails-to-preserve`,
non-blocking per skills/manager/phases/findings.md's ranking table -- can ship behind this
fragment). The auditor could not construct a concrete trigger for ini_get_all(null, true)
returning false in this daemon's own runtime (plain CLI SAPI, no extension name argument ever
passed), so likelihood is asserted as reachable but not demonstrated -- treat as low until
someone names an input that reaches it.

Not fixed in the #125/#126 lane: no design decision was made about whether this warrants a
distinguishable failure mode (e.g. throwing, or logging a warning) versus staying silent, and
the underlying risk was judged low enough that guessing at one felt worse than leaving it
documented here.
