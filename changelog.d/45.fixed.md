- Fixed (#45): the no-pcntl cold path no longer hangs forever when a `rector.php` bootstrap writes more than one pipe buffer's worth to stderr (PHP notices, deprecations). `RectorRunner::runCold()` used to read the cold child's stdout and stderr one after another with `stream_get_contents()`; once either pipe filled its OS buffer before reaching EOF, the parent and the child deadlocked, wedging the whole single-threaded stdio server. Both pipes are now drained concurrently with `stream_select()`.
